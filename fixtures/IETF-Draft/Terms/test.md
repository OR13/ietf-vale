# Terms

The IPSEC and IPSec spellings are wrong, as are e-mail, E-mail, on-line,
time-stamp, sub-domain, multi-homed, multi-homing, pseudo-wire, DiffServ,
demultiplexor, US-ASCII, and Internet Draft.

These are correct and must stay clean: IPsec, email, online, timestamp,
subdomain, multihomed, multihoming, pseudowire, Diffserv, demultiplexer, ASCII,
and Internet-Draft.

The Terms list defers to the document on Acknowledgements and Acknowledgments,
so neither spelling is flagged here.
