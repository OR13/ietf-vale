# Concision

These have a shorter form: in order to negotiate, prior to the handshake, due to
the fact that the timer expired, in the event that the peer is absent, it is
important to note that the flag is optional, and the client is able to retry.

These must stay clean: to negotiate, before the handshake, because the timer
expired, if the peer is absent, note that the flag is optional, and the client
can retry.

Longer explanation is legitimate under BCP 22 and is not flagged: this section
describes the reasoning behind the design, the alternatives the working group
considered, and why they were rejected.
