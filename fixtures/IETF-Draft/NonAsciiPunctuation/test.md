# NonAsciiPunctuation

Smart punctuation is wrong: “quoted”, it’s, an em dash — here, an en dash – here, and an ellipsis … here.

These must stay clean: "quoted", it's, a parenthetical (like this one), a
hyphenated compound such as well-known, and three dots ... here.

Non-ASCII letters are permitted by RFC 7997 and are not punctuation: Rencóntre.
